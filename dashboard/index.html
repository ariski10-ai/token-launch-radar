How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth × launch realism

    The category multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of this snapshot's own tokenised protocols in the same category, so the market prices the unit of TVL, not a hand-kept list. Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked; they sit in the comparables strip as the model's calibration set.
